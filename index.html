About ChrisDaChiro Therapy Tools

Built to help you in and out of the office

“We take care of other people every day. When are we going to take care of ourselves?”

ChrisDaChiro Therapy Tools was created as a way for me to help people in and out of the office, giving them a way to ease soreness, fatigue and tension wherever they go.

Inspired by my work in the chiropractic field and by making an impact in communities through health, movement and wellness, I wanted to create a brand that helps people take a more active role in their everyday routines.

Whether we know it or not, we put strain and stress on our bodies, physically and mentally, every day. My goal is to provide productive and cost-effective therapeutic tools to ease that strain and tension.

No matter your career field or profession, ChrisDaChiro Therapy Tools is here to help you take care of YOU.

Who are these tools for?

Anyone who carries everyday tension: desk workers, healthcare workers, athletes, parents, drivers, tradespeople. If your body works hard, these tools are for you.

How do I use the Cervical Traction Pillow?

Lie on your back with the base of your skull resting in the contoured curve. Start with 5–10 minutes and build up gradually. Stop if anything feels painful.

Is the massage gun safe to use anywhere?

Use it on muscle, not on bone, joints, the front of the neck or the head. Keep it moving and start on the lowest setting.
